Hayekian Postiz

Privacy policy

Hayekian Postiz is a private, self-hosted social publishing workspace operated by Jeff Graber.

Google and YouTube data

The workspace uses YouTube API Services. When an operator connects YouTube, Postiz uses Google OAuth to identify the consenting account and the selected channel. It can read account and channel details such as the display name, image, and channel identifier, as well as video metadata and analytics. Postiz stores the connection record and the access and refresh credentials needed to operate the integration. These credentials are sent to Google when required to authenticate or renew authorization. See the Google Privacy Policy for Google's handling of information.

How the workspace uses data

Authorized operators use the integration to identify connected channels, view publishing status and analytics, prepare video metadata, and upload or update videos on the selected channel after approval. Videos and accompanying text entered into the workspace are stored to support those operations. A workspace assistant authorized to perform a channel lookup may receive channel labels and Postiz connection identifiers.

The workspace limits its use of Google user data to these functions. It does not permit the sale of that data, use for advertising, or use to train generalized AI or machine learning models. The Google API Services User Data Policy and its Limited Use requirements apply to information received from Google APIs.

Access and deletion

Google data associated with a connection is available to authorized workspace operators for the functions described above. You can revoke the application's Google access in your Google Account permissions. To request a copy or deletion of data held by the workspace, or removal of a Postiz connection, email contact@jeffgraber.com. Deleting data from this workspace leaves copies on YouTube under your control in YouTube Studio.
