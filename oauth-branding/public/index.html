Hayekian Postiz

Hayekian Postiz is a private social publishing workspace operated by Jeff Graber and powered by self-hosted Postiz. It helps authorized operators prepare videos and manage approved publishing for the YouTube channels they administer.

Google authorization lets the workspace identify the channel the operator selects, read channel information and analytics, and upload or update a video when an operator approves a publishing action.
